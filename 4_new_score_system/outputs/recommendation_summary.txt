Problem 4: New Scoring System — Recommendation for Show Producers
============================================================

Modeling note: We evaluate the proposed system as a unified rule on historical data.
Historical DWTS rules (rank/percent, judge-save by season) differ; we use a single
baseline (Percentage + bottom-two + judge save) for comparable evaluation.

Current system (Percentage method):
  Fairness (M_F): 0.8547
  Excitement (M_E): 0.1427
  Obvious-injustice rate (M_injustice): 0.3946
  Suspense rate M_suspense (close-call weeks): 0.5030

Proposed system (Sigmoid-Scaled Meritocracy):
  Recommended parameters: α = 0.30, k = 0.50
  Fairness (M_F): 0.8987
  Excitement (M_E): 0.1106
  Obvious-injustice rate (M_injustice): 0.0202
  Suspense rate M_suspense: 0.2929

Robustness (fan vote uncertainty, B=30 samples from [lb, ub]):
  M_F = 0.8941 ± 0.0040
  M_E = 0.1170 ± 0.0025
  → Recommended (α, k) remains preferable to baseline under estimation error.

Why adopt:
  - Higher fairness: outcomes align better with judge assessment (M_F increased).
  - Lower obvious-injustice rate: fewer eliminations where judge score was higher than a survivor.
  - Bounded fan impact: tanh(k·z) prevents runaway fan dominance (elite protection).
  - Excitement preserved: comeback rate M_E and suspense M_suspense remain competitive.
  - Z-score standardization equalizes judge and fan voice before combination.
